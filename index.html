How it works

  • 1Eight companies come to you for help. Three things could be wrong with each one, and one of them really is.
  • 2First you read the numbers. Four rounds show a funnel (people moving through steps). Four show a P&L (where the money goes).
  • 3You get one free question per round. It shows what is normal for that kind of company, for one number.
  • 4Then you bet 100 chips on what is wrong. Sure? Go all in. Not sure? Split them.
  • 5All 100 chips on the real problem scores 100 points. The best possible score is 800.
